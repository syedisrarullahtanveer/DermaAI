# Clinical Safety & Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) Guidelines

## 1. Intended Use
DermaAI is an investigational Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) intended to assist qualified dermatologists in assessing dermatoscopic images of pigmented skin lesions.

## 2. Regulatory Boundaries
- **Non-Autonomous:** DermaAI shall NOT be used as an autonomous diagnostic device.
- **Biopsy Recommendation:** Any lesion flagging High or Critical risk must undergo standard clinical dermoscopic evaluation and histological confirmation.
- **Exclusion Criteria:** Images with extensive ulceration, non-dermatoscopic macroscopic photos, or subungual lesions have not been validated.
