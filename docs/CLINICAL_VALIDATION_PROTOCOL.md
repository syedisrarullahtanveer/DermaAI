# Multi-Center Clinical Validation Protocol

## 1. Study Objective
Evaluate diagnostic sensitivity and specificity of DermaAI against histological gold standards across independent university hospital cohorts.

## 2. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria
- **Inclusion:** Adult patients presenting with pigmented skin lesions scheduled for biopsy.
- **Exclusion:** Mucosal, subungual, or previous cryotherapy lesions.

## 3. Endpoints
- Primary: Melanoma sensitivity at fixed 80% specificity.
- Secondary: Reduction in unnecessary biopsy rate for benign nevi.
